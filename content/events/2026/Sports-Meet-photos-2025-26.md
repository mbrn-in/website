The Annual Sports and Cultural Meet of the Sri Sathya Sai Institute of Higher Learning was a vibrant celebration of talent, discipline, and values-based education. Students from all campuses showcased their skills through synchronized sports events, cultural performances, music, and drama. The programme reflected unity, teamwork, and devotion, highlighting holistic development of body, mind, and spirit. Held at Prasanthi Nilayam, the meet concluded with a prize distribution ceremony, offering heartfelt tributes and reinforcing the institute’s commitment to excellence grounded in human values.
